## 6. Assumptions still needing validation (interviews and workshops)

1. **[Assumption]** Families often do not know a case's status between reporting and court. *Validate with:* survivor-support NGOs, probation officers, Sauti 116 call themes.
2. **[Assumption]** Referral handoffs (police → health → probation → ODPP) are tracked on paper or by phone, and acknowledgement is not recorded. *Validate with:* CFPU officers, health workers, State Attorneys.
3. **[Assumption]** Children are asked to repeat their account to several institutions. *Validate with:* institutional SOPs and practitioners.
4. **[Assumption]** Institutions would accept a coordination layer next to their own systems. *Validate with:* stakeholders in the Administration of Justice programme (formerly JLOS).
5. **[Assumption]** Feature-phone USSD reaches carers who have no smartphones. *Validate with:* UCC/UBOS ICT access data and pilot usage.

## Research questions

1. Where do delays happen in a child victim's journey between report, medical care, prosecution review and hearing?
2. Which handoffs lack an acknowledgement or feedback loop?
3. What information do children and caregivers need, in which language and through which channel, to feel informed and safe?
4. What is the minimum information each institution needs to act on a referral?
5. Which safeguards stop family access or notifications from putting a child at risk?
